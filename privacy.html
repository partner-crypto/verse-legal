Verse: Memorize Scripture

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Verse is a Bible verse memorization app. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We collect the minimum we need to run the app and never sell your personal data.

Information we collect

Category What it includes
Account information Email address and a securely hashed password (or your Sign in with Apple identifier) when you create an account.
Memorization activity Verses you've added, review history, streaks, mastery levels, and points — used to power your calendar, analytics, and the leaderboard.
Subscription status Whether you have an active Verse Plus subscription, managed through Apple's App Store — we do not receive or store your payment card details.
Usage data Basic app diagnostics (crash reports, feature usage) to help us fix bugs and improve the app.

How we use your information

  • To sync your verses, progress, and streaks across your devices.
  • To power features like your memorization calendar, analytics, and AI-driven coaching insights.
  • To display your rank on the leaderboard, if you choose to participate.
  • To validate your Verse Plus subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app performance and reliability.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not show third-party advertising in Verse.
  • We do not share your memorization activity publicly beyond the display name and rank you choose to show on the leaderboard.

Third-party services

Verse uses a small number of trusted service providers to operate the app, including:

  • A backend and authentication provider to store your account and verse data securely.
  • Apple's App Store and StoreKit to process subscriptions.
  • An AI provider to generate the optional coaching insights in the app, using your memorization activity.

These providers only receive the data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing support@getverse.app. We'll complete the deletion within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Verse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your account data at any time by contacting support.
  • You can opt out of leaderboard visibility in your profile settings.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime through your Apple ID settings.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@getverse.app.
