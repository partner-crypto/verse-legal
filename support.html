Frequently asked questions

How do I start memorizing a verse?

From the Home tab, tap any verse in your queue and choose "Memorize it now." You'll work through it using the word-bank recall drill, tapping the words in order as you recall them.

How does Verse decide when to review a verse again?

Verse uses spaced repetition — verses you know well are shown less often, and ones you're still shaky on come back sooner. You can see everything that's due today from the Home tab.

What's included in Verse Plus?

Verse Plus unlocks unlimited verses and lists, full access to your memorization calendar and analytics, the leaderboard, and AI-powered coaching insights on your progress. A 7-day free trial is included with both the monthly and yearly plans.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, select Verse, and choose Cancel Subscription. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Can I use my own translation or verse list?

Verse currently supports a library of over 31,000 verses across standard translations. Custom translation support and importing your own lists are on our roadmap.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear it — email us at support@getverse.app with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what you expected, and a screenshot if possible).

How do I delete my account and data?

Email support@getverse.app from the address associated with your account and we'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
